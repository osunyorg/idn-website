We're a grassroots online movement that aims to raise awareness of the harms of Big Tech and AI, and to critique the existing response to the Big Tech and AI movements, discussing what it would mean to genuinely move away from them (not to simply want to reform, or take control, of existing tech platforms), and to create alternative worlds free of oppressive and harmful technology for everyone.
