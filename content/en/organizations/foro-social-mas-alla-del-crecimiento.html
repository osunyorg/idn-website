The Foro Social Más Allá del Crecimiento (Beyond Growth Social Forum) is a space for deliberation and democratic decision-making for a new economic model beyond growth and beyond capitalism. The Social Forum process emerged from the Beyond Growth Conference, which took place in September 2025 in the Spanish Parliament, and held its first session in February 2026 at the Autonomous University of Madrid. To date, more than 140 organizations, networks, and alliances have joined the process.
